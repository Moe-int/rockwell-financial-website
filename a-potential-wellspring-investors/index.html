A Potential Wellspring Investors

By Rockwell FinancialFebruary 14, 2025

While the spotlight currently shines on booming property markets like Brisbane and Perth, astute investors shouldn’t overlook the quiet potential brewing in Melbourne and Victoria.

Despite recent slowdowns, several factors converge to make Melbourne a compelling long-term play for capital growth.

For starters, unlike other capitals experiencing explosive price surges, Melbourne has seen a more measured growth trajectory over the past seven years. This means property values haven’t reached inflated levels, leaving room for significant future appreciation.

A projected increase in international migration, particularly drawn to Melbourne’s vibrant lifestyle and diverse communities, is set to fuel increasing demand for housing. This demand-supply dynamic favors upward pressure on property prices.

Furthermore, the current market dip presents a strategic entry point for investors. December’s price decline, pushing Melbourne’s median dwelling value below Brisbane’s for the first time since 2009, signals a potential buying window before the next upswing. This window of opportunity is further amplified by the anticipation of a cash rate cut later this year.

“Experts like Eliza Owen of CoreLogic believe such a cut could trigger a significant rebound in Melbourne’s housing market, with the first half of 2024 offering prime buying opportunities for investors ahead of the anticipated recovery.”

Beyond immediate market dynamics, Melbourne boasts long-term stability factors that underpin its property value potential. As Australia’s second-largest city, it possesses robust infrastructure, a diversified economy, and a track record of attracting businesses and migration. This inherent stability provides a firm foundation for sustained property value growth over the long term.

“Industry figures like Terry Ryder of Hotspotting are also observing rising demand trends, suggesting a shift towards positive momentum in the Melbourne market.”

However, they advise caution regarding potential state government policies that could impact investors. This highlights the importance of conducting thorough research and working with market experts to understand market nuances and choosing the right locations before making investment decisions.

While short-term fluctuations are always a possibility, the combination of subdued recent growth, projected demand increase, early entry opportunity, and long-term stability make Melbourne a city worth considering for investors ready to enter the market looking for medium-to-long-term capital gains.

By carefully navigating the market with informed choices and expert guidance, Melbourne could indeed reward patient investors with substantial capital gains in the years to come, as it has done for the last 60 years at an average capital growth rate of 8.94%.
