Melbourne Crowned Best City in the World. Here's What That Means for Property Investors

By Rockwell FinancialApril 17, 2026

In 2026, Melbourne was ranked the best city in the world by Time Out.

For long-term Melbourne property investment, that kind of recognition is more than just a headline. It is a demand signal worth paying attention to.

How Melbourne made history

Time Out surveyed more than 24,000 locals across 42 languages, rating their city on everything from affordability and happiness, to food and cultural life. For the first time ever, an Australian city topped the list, beating global powerhouses Shanghai, Edinburgh, London and New York.

The next closest Australian cities? Sydney at #21 and Adelaide at #29.

This was not a fluke. Melbourne ranked #2 in 2016 and #4 as recently as 2025. This is a city that people consistently choose, and that consistency matters.

What does being the world’s best city mean for Melbourne property investment?

Cities people consistently choose to live in drive demand. And demand is what matters for property investors.

When a city is recognised globally for liveability, affordability and cultural life, it attracts more people such as interstate movers, overseas migrants, students, and professionals. More people needing homes means more pressure on an already tight rental market.

Melbourne’s rental vacancy rate currently sits at just 1.1%, well below the 3% considered balanced. A #1 global ranking does not ease that pressure. It adds to it.

The Melbourne property market in 2026 — what the data says

This is not a one-off result

Melbourne has placed in the top five of this ranking three times in the past decade. That kind of consistency reflects something structural about the city — its liveability, its infrastructure, its population growth, and its ongoing appeal to people from all over the world.

Victoria attracts nearly 30% of all overseas migrants to Australia, with Melbourne receiving the highest overseas migration intake of any city in the country. A city that people actively choose,  and keep choosing, is a city where housing demand is not slowing down anytime soon.
