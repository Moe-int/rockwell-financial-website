What is Equity? A Simple Explanation for Australian Property Investors.

By Rockwell FinancialApril 24, 2026

You have probably heard the word before. But what does equity in property investment actually mean for Australian families? It is simpler than you think, and understanding it is one of the most important steps you can take before considering an investment property.

What is equity in property?

Equity is the difference between what your property is worth and what you still owe on it.

Here is a simple example. If your home is worth $600,000 and you owe $350,000 on your mortgage, your equity is $250,000.

That is it. No complicated formula. No financial degree required.

Why does equity matter for property investors in Australia?

Because equity can help you take the next step. Instead of saving a full deposit from scratch, many families use the equity in their current home to help buy an investment property.

You do not need to be a finance expert to understand this. You just need someone who can explain it clearly and show you how it could work for your family.

How does equity in property investment grow over time?

Equity grows in two ways.

First, when your property increases in value over time. Second, as you pay down your mortgage.

Both can contribute to growing your equity position over time. And over time, they can open up options you might not have realised you had.
