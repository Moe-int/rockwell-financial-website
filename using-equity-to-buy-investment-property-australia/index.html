How to Use Equity to Buy an Investment Property in Australia.

By Rockwell FinancialApril 27, 2026

Ever heard someone say “just use your equity” and wondered what that actually means? Using equity to buy an investment property in Australia is one of the most common ways everyday families get started, and it is simpler than most people think.

What is equity and how does it work?

Equity is simply the difference between what your home is worth and what you still owe on it.

For example, your home is worth $800,000. You owe $400,000 on your mortgage. That means you have $400,000 in equity.

How much equity can you actually access?

You cannot access all of it. Most lenders will let you borrow up to 80% of your home’s value. In this example, that is $640,000. Take away the $400,000 you still owe, and you could potentially access up to $240,000.

That $240,000 could be used as a deposit toward buying an investment property, without touching your savings account.

That is how a lot of Australian families start their property investment journey. Not by saving up a large deposit over years, but by using the value that is already sitting in their home.

Why using equity to buy an investment property in Australia requires the right structure

Here is the important part. The wrong loan setup can quietly cost your family thousands. Whereas the right one can put you in a much stronger position from day one.

Equity is not just a number. It is a tool, and like any tool, the result depends on how it is used.

How Rockwell Financial helps families understand their equity position

At Rockwell Financial, we help families understand their equity position and determine whether using it to invest is the right move for their situation.
