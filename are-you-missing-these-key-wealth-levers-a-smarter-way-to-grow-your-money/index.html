Are You Missing These Key Wealth Levers?

By Rockwell FinancialJune 13, 2025
When it comes to building wealth, most people focus on two things: earning more or spending less. But those aren’t the only levers available — and they’re often the slowest.
Here are five practical levers we help our clients activate to accelerate their financial progress:
  1. Equity Leverage

If you own a home, chances are you’re sitting on untapped equity.
What to do:

  • Get a valuation and review your borrowing capacity.
  • Explore borrowing to invest in a high-growth property.
  • Make sure the debt is structured to support investment, not consumption.
  1. Debt Efficiency

Every dollar in interest you save is a dollar you can invest.
What to do:

  • Use offset accounts to reduce interest.
  • Refinance to a more competitive loan structure.
  • Consider debt recycling to convert bad debt into deductible debt.
  1. Tax Optimisation (with the right advice)

Tax can either slow you down or work in your favour.
What to do:

  • Speak to your accountant about deductions, depreciation, and loan structures.
  • Use tax returns and savings to fund new investments or reduce non-deductible debt.
  1. Superannuation Strategy

Your super is more powerful than you think — and not just for retirement.
What to do:

  • Review your fund’s performance and fees.
  • Explore if an SMSF could give you more control (see Article 5).
  • Align your super with your broader investment goals.
  1. Goal-Driven Planning

Without direction, even high income won’t get you to financial independence.
What to do:

  • Map out your retirement timeline and desired passive income.
  • Use forecasting tools to build a property and lending roadmap.
  • Review and adjust annually.

Key Takeaway: Wealth isn’t just about working harder — it’s about working smarter with what you’ve got. If you’re not using these five levers, you’re leaving money on the table.

General Warning: This information is for general purposes only and is not personal financial advice. Seek professional guidance before making financial decisions.
