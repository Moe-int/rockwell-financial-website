Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

How we collect, use and protect your information, and the terms on which we provide our services. Last updated October 2026.

Privacy Policy

We collect personal information reasonably necessary to deliver our property investment consultancy services and coordinate support from your nominated independent delivery partners.

This may include:

  • Your name, contact details and identification documents.
  • Financial information, including income, assets, liabilities and credit history.
  • Employment information.
  • Your circumstances, investment goals and objectives.
  • Meeting notes, recordings and transcripts, where applicable.

We generally collect this information directly from you, including through enquiries, forms, telephone calls and meetings. We may also receive relevant information from your nominated delivery partners where authorised or otherwise permitted by law.

We use your information to:

  • Understand your circumstances and goals and provide our property investment consultancy services.
  • Connect you with accredited financial advisers, finance brokers and other nominated service partners where relevant.
  • Coordinate with your nominated finance partners to assist with applications through accredited providers.
  • Communicate with you about our services and manage appointments and administration.
  • Prepare meeting notes and support quality assurance and staff training.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

If you choose not to provide information we reasonably need, we may be unable to provide some services or coordinate assistance from delivery partners.

We may share relevant personal information with your nominated independent delivery partners, including finance, legal, financial advice and property management professionals, to provide or coordinate the services you request.

We may also share relevant information with technology providers and other service providers that support our systems, communications and administration, or where disclosure is required or permitted by law. Where required, we will seek your consent before sharing your information.

Independent delivery partners maintain their own privacy obligations and may handle your information under their own privacy policies.

We use technology to deliver our services, manage client information and communications, and support administration, quality assurance and training. This may include client relationship management (CRM) systems, recording, transcription and AI-assisted tools. These systems may store and process personal information, including contact details, financial information, meeting notes, recordings and transcripts, where applicable. Some technology providers may store or process this information overseas.

We will let you know when a session is to be recorded. Please contact our office if you have questions or concerns about how we use technology to handle your information.

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure.

We also take reasonable steps to destroy or de-identify personal information when it is no longer needed, unless we are required or permitted by law to retain it.

We may contact you by email, SMS or other electronic means. While we take reasonable steps to protect electronic communications, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you or ask us to correct information that is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant or misleading. Please contact our office using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding. If we cannot fulfil your request, we will explain why, subject to applicable legal requirements.

If you have a concern or complaint about how we handle your personal information, please contact our office with the relevant details.

We will investigate and respond within a reasonable timeframe.

For privacy enquiries, access or correction requests, or complaints, please contact:

Rockwell Financial Email: info@rockwellfinancial.com.au

By using our services, you consent to the collection, use, and disclosure of your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to our practices or legal requirements. The current version will be available on our website.

Terms of Service

Rockwell Financial provides property investment consultancy services, including education, research, property strategy preparation and property sourcing.

We collect information about your circumstances and goals to support these services and coordinate assistance from your nominated independent delivery partners.

We do not provide financial, tax or legal advice. Where required, we may refer or introduce you to appropriately qualified financial advisers, finance brokers, tax advisers, legal practitioners or other professionals. Specialist advice and services are provided separately by those professionals under their own licences, qualifications and obligations, as applicable.

Any financial information provided by Rockwell Financial is general in nature and for educational purposes only. It does not constitute or replace professional financial, tax or legal advice tailored to your circumstances.

Any calculations, examples or projections are illustrative only, rely on assumptions and are not guarantees of future outcomes. Actual outcomes may differ.

Before acting on any financial information, you should consider whether it is appropriate for your circumstances and seek advice from an appropriately qualified professional. Specialist advice is provided separately by the relevant professional under their own licences, qualifications and obligations, as applicable.

While we provide our services with care and diligence, we do not guarantee investment outcomes, financial returns, property values, rental income, tax benefits or future performance.

Property investment involves risks, and outcomes depend on market conditions, costs and individual circumstances.

We may introduce you to independent delivery partners to assist with your property investment journey. You are welcome to use your own preferred providers, and we will coordinate with them where appropriate.

Third-party services may be subject to separate agreements, fees, eligibility requirements and privacy policies. Each provider is responsible for the advice and services they deliver.

You are responsible for:

  • Providing accurate and up-to-date information to Rockwell Financial and your nominated delivery partners.
  • Notifying us of relevant changes to your circumstances.
  • Reviewing information and assumptions relevant to your decisions.
  • Obtaining appropriate independent financial, tax and legal advice before making investment decisions.
  • Reviewing and understanding any separate agreements with delivery partners.

Any applicable fees, payment requirements, cancellation terms or booking conditions will be provided to you before you agree to the relevant service or appointment.

Nothing in these terms excludes, restricts or modifies any right, guarantee or remedy under the Australian Consumer Law or other applicable law that cannot lawfully be excluded, restricted or modified.

Subject to those rights, Rockwell Financial’s responsibility for any loss or damage will depend on the circumstances and applicable law. Independent delivery partners are responsible for their own advice, services and conduct.

We handle personal information as described in our Privacy Policy above.
