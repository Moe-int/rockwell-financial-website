Australia's Townhouse Hotspots for 2026: Where the Data is Pointing.

By Rockwell FinancialJune 29, 2026

New ABS data is revealing the townhouse hotspots Australia 2026 investors are watching, and the results might surprise you.

Melbourne leads the way but other cities are closing the gap fast.

Where are Australia’s townhouse hotspots in 2026?

ABS building approval data shows Melbourne West received more townhouse building approvals than any other location in Australia. That being, 2,196 approvals in the twelve months to April 2026. Melbourne regions take out all three of the top spots on the list.

But other cities are rising quickly. Here is how the top locations ranked:

  • 1st — Melbourne West, VIC: 2,196 approvals
  • 2nd and 3rd — Melbourne regions (VIC)
  • 4th — Logan Beaudesert, QLD: 1,254 approvals (up 147%)
  • 5th — Adelaide West, SA: 1,142 approvals (up 50%)
  • 7th — Sydney South West, NSW: 1,052 approvals

Regional cities are also growing in popularity. The Hunter Valley, Gold Coast and Illawarra all feature in the top twenty locations nationally.

Why are townhouse building approvals rising across Australia?

Many state governments have been changing zoning restrictions to allow townhouses, which has resulted in a significant increase in approvals nationally. It is a policy-driven shift that is opening up new pockets of supply across the country.

The Logan Beaudesert result — a 147% increase in approvals — is a clear example of what happens when zoning changes unlock previously restricted land. Adelaide West’s 50% increase tells a similar story.

What does townhouse approval data mean for property investors in Australia?

Building approval data is one of the most reliable leading indicators in property research. Where approvals are rising, developers are responding to demand. And where demand for a property type is rising, it is worth understanding why.

Townhouses sit at a practical price point for many Australian families. They are typically more affordable than detached houses in the same suburb, while offering more space and privacy than apartments. For investors, they can offer a compelling combination of lower entry cost and strong rental appeal.

The Melbourne West result is particularly interesting in the context of the broader Melbourne story. Victoria’s infrastructure pipeline, its migration intake and its affordability relative to Sydney are already drawing investor attention. Townhouse approval data adds another layer to that picture.

Townhouse hotspots Australia 2026: what it means for your long-term property strategy

Approval data tells you where supply is going. It does not tell you what is right for your family. Those are two different questions.

A suburb with high townhouse approvals might signal strong long-term demand. Or it might signal oversupply risk if approvals outpace population growth. Understanding the difference is where the right guidance matters.
