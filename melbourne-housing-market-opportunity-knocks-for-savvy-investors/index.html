Melbourne Housing Market: Opportunity Knocks for Savvy Investors

By Rockwell FinancialFebruary 17, 2025

Opportunity is now for ready investors looking for longer-term capital growth.

Potential interest rate cuts in 2024/25 and a growing population is likely to contribute to a price rebound, potentially placing Melbourne among Australia’s best performers for medium-to-long-term capital gains. This follows seven years of subdued growth and suggests rising demand could create favourable conditions for investors looking to enter the market now.

Let’s look at the factors influencing Melbourne’s property market.

Factors Favouring Melbourne

Price Correction

Stable prices have made Melbourne properties more attractively priced compared to historic highs. This creates an entry point for investors before a predicted rebound.

Pent-Up Demand

Unlike other capital cities that experienced significant price surges during the pandemic, Melbourne’s market remained comparatively stable. This pent-up demand is expected to fuel market growth as economic conditions become more favourable over the next few years.


Expected Migration Boost

Australia’s anticipated increase in international migration is likely to benefit Melbourne, with its vibrant lifestyle and diverse job market drawing new residents and boosting housing demand.

Second-City Security

As Australia’s second-largest city, Melbourne boasts strong infrastructure, a diversified economy, and a long-term growth story. This offers investors a measure of stability and risk mitigation compared to smaller markets.

Capitalizing on the Potential

Early Entry Advantage

The first half of 2024 presents an advantageous window for investment, allowing investors to secure properties before a broader market upswing.

Interest Rate Outlook

Potential future interest rate cuts bolstered by a sharply falling inflation rate could further stimulate the real estate markets growth.


Targeted Investment

While overall market recovery is expected, identifying undervalued pockets within the broader Melbourne region, such as the far west and outer north suburbs, could offer additional return potential. However, careful analysis and expert guidance are crucial in navigating these areas for the best return and more secure investments.

Policy Considerations

There have been recent changes to some government policies impacting the rental. While these changes won’t affect many investors, understanding these policy nuances and their potential impact on specific segments of the market is essential for informed investment decisions.

Professional Guidance

Engaging qualified property experts can help investors identify optimal investment opportunities, navigate market complexities, and mitigate potential risks.

Melbourne’s housing market presents a well-rounded investment proposition for long-term investors. Melbourne’s strong fundamentals and growth potential offer fertile ground, especially for those who invest early, strategically, and with expert guidance. In essence, think marathon, not sprint – the rewards may unfold gradually, but the finish line could be golden.
