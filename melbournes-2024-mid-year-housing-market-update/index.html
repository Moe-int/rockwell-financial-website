Melbourne’s 2024 Mid-Year Housing Market Update

By Rockwell FinancialFebruary 14, 2025

The savvy property investor knows one key truth: it’s all about timing.

Forget chasing overheated markets – the real magic happens by identifying locations primed for long-term growth before prices explode.

This is where Melbourne shines. While other cities have seen recent price surges, Melbourne presents a unique opportunity.

Melbourne: Perfectly Positioned for Takeoff

Imagine Perth a few years ago, right before its impressive rise. That’s exactly where Melbourne sits now.

Victoria boasts one of Australia’s strongest economies, fueled by booming population growth – thanks to immigration and student influxes. ABS data confirms this trend, with Melbourne’s population growth exceeding the national average by a full percentage point last year.

What’s even more exciting is that Melbourne hasn’t yet experienced the dramatic price hikes seen elsewhere. Instead, there’s been a surge in buyer activity, a telltale sign of a market about to heat up.

Unlocking Investment Gems in Melbourne

While Melbourne as a whole offer’s fantastic potential, some suburbs are poised for even greater success.

Here are a few of Melbourne’s leading picks…

City of Melton: This location offers a dream combination – affordable real estate and easy access to major employment hubs. Median property prices start at a very attractive $470,000, making it a fantastic entry point for investors. With significant population growth on the horizon, fueled by infrastructure upgrades and a planned $1 billion hospital, Melton promises strong returns on both capital growth and rental income.

City of Whittlesea: This diverse and rapidly growing municipality is another Melbourne gem. Whittlesea is experiencing significant investment in infrastructure, transportation, and commercial development, creating jobs and driving demand for housing. Major projects like the new city of Cloverton and the $5 billion La Trobe University development are sure to attract a wave of residents, further tightening the already low vacancy rates.

Strategic Suburbs Offer Entry Points

Don’t be fooled by Melbourne’s expensive reputation. The city boasts a range of suburbs offering excellent value for investors. Areas like Melbourne’s west and north-west present a chance to acquire houses at a median price of $699,117 and $756,176 respectively, with some key areas offering even lower entry points. These areas are poised for growth as Melbourne expands and infrastructure development progresses.

The Melbourne Advantage: Growth, Affordability, and Lifestyle

Melbourne offers a compelling proposition for investors seeking strategic property buys.

Here’s what sets it apart:

  • Strong Fundamentals: A robust economy, booming population, and ongoing infrastructure investment create a solid foundation for future growth.
  • Affordability: Compared to other major cities, Melbourne offers much better value for your investment dollar.
  • Lifestyle Appeal: Melbourne’s vibrant culture, diverse neighborhoods, and proximity to nature make it a highly desirable place to live, ensuring a steady stream of renters for your property.

By investing in Melbourne now, you’re positioning yourself to capitalize on a market on the cusp of significant growth. It’s a smart move that will deliver strong returns for years to come.
